# AstroImageNow v1.14 — Travel comparison and a concise night plan

Baseline: v1.13.0, `a0cadba980138d1f31faf5c586848ed05012c74e`. Implementation branch: `codex/travel-night-plan`. Jason explicitly authorized the combined implementation and deployment on September 21, 2026.

## Result

The normal refresh updates Home and JGAP together, reusing the active-site request. The fixed pair resolves existing IDs/names; missing or ambiguous profiles remain explicit. Other saved sites and all credentials, coordinates, Bortle values, journal entries and plan IDs are preserved. Selecting another freshly cached site does not make another request. Target, date, rig, mode and filter selection stay local. A repeated failed refresh after a coordinate/context change cannot reuse another context's last-good forecast.

Travel Site Comparison replaces the manual refresh button and explanation with two compact cards: site, Bortle, supported window or concise limitation, and expandable geometry. Forecast details holds provider timestamps, supporting weather measurements, model/credits, diagnostics and the update check. A muted linked source line remains beside the weather; the [official Foreca attribution requirements](https://business.foreca.com/hubfs/logo/Foreca%20Logo%20and%20Attribution%20Requirements.pdf) explicitly allow name/text attribution. Backups retain their actual source name. Meaningful errors and uncertainty remain visible.

The existing Imaging Targets panel now synthesizes Home, JGAP, neither, or an undecided comparison. A primary target and useful alternative include the proposed rig, filter and matching window. Use this plan selects the site and setup, without saving a session or planned target automatically. Suggestions run entirely on local rules and existing provider data; there are no remote LLM requests or new services.

## Decision rules and boundaries

- Require 60 minutes of usable deep-sky time or 20 minutes for Moon/planets. A short interval of fully supported conditions cannot conceal a longer practical interval with caution.
- Exclude unknown framing and mosaics from automatic deep-sky setup plans. Manual exploration remains available.
- Prefer Home when opportunities are comparable. Travel can win when Home has no practical match, or for the same target/optics with at least 90 extra minutes of deep-sky time (60 for planets). Faint targets can favor a site at least two Bortle classes darker if the window is at least two hours, broadly comparable, and free of a modeled Moon caution. Specialized forecast support cannot be worse than the competing Home recommendation. Broadband filter differences do not prevent a comparison; dual-band and broadband are not treated as equivalent.
- These are transparent planning heuristics, not modeled SNR, calibrated success probabilities, driving-time optimization or site-access advice. Bright planets receive no automatic darker-site advantage. An estimated window keeps its ≈ marker; seeing/transparency are never invented.
- A selected L-Ultimate is not endorsed for broadband or lunar/planetary targets. L-Pro is not endorsed for natural-color planetary capture. Manual filter advice remains visible. Planned/proposed ownership and unverified C8/UltraCat commissioning stay explicit.
- Both sites must have enough current data to judge a travel choice. Pending, stale or incomplete comparison data do not become “neither,” a preferred site, or a setup go-ahead.
- The existing general conditions score is unchanged and independent of site-selection advice, target, rig, filter, and Moon. Logged outcomes retain only their modest ranking influence; no entries means unknown history.
