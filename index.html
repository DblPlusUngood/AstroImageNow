      Sources and planning limits

      Coordinates and catalog dimensions: OpenNGC, Mattia Verga and contributors, adapted under CC BY-SA 4.0. Geometry: Astronomy Engine. Data and method notes.

      Sizes are approximate and may describe only part of a complex. Framing assumes nominal optics and deliberate camera rotation; inspect a real survey image before final framing. Moon and site guidance are heuristics, not exposure predictions. C8, Z73 and UltraCat fields are nominal; physical adapter compatibility and commissioning are separate. Planet positions change with the observing time. Disk size, video cropping, obstructions, field rotation, special events are not yet modeled. Only sessions entered in this device’s journal influence capture-history preferences. Forecast thresholds are planning heuristics, not measured imaging-success probabilities. Suggestions run locally. Shared cameras and mounts are alternative setups. Night plans require at least 60 minutes for deep sky or 20 minutes for planets; automatic plans exclude unknown framing and mosaics. Home is preferred when opportunities are comparable. Travel requires a practical advantage with comparable forecast support: a longer window, or darker skies for a faint target without a Moon caution. Drive time, access and local obstructions are not assessed.

      Scoring details
      The conditions score uses available Astrospheric and weather data. Target, rig, filter and Moon context are handled in Imaging Targets; they do not change this score.
